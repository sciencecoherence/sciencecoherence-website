Formal Deductions

Key theorems of the architecture

From the single axiom that coherence is alignment across relations, five mathematical statements govern the physical and phenomenological dynamics of the closed recursion.

Theorem 01 · Dynamical Closure
$$\nabla\Phi \longrightarrow \Lambda \longrightarrow \Omega \longrightarrow \Delta, \quad \Delta \rightsquigarrow \Lambda, \quad \Omega \rightsquigarrow \nabla\Phi$$

Reality is determined by the closure of the loop itself. The next state arises through self-referential action: $\Omega(t+\delta t)|\tilde{\psi}(t+\delta t)\rangle = \Delta(t+\delta t)\Lambda(t+\delta t)\nabla(t+\delta t)|\psi(t)\rangle$. No outside clock or judge.

Theorem 02 · Radiative Carrier
$$\Psi := W_\Omega(\Delta), \quad \Psi \rightsquigarrow \Delta$$

Every local act of integration produces a propagating, phase-carrying wave pattern $\Psi$. Waves from prior integrations couple back to bias future possibilities: what has been integrated continues participating in the world.

Theorem 03 · Covariant Transport
$$\nabla_\mu = \partial_\mu - iA_\mu, \quad \nabla\Psi = 0 \quad (\text{with } \Omega \subset \nabla C)$$

Temporal phase-locking and spatial phase-order unify into parallel transport of coherence. This is the kinematic starting point for emergent gauge interactions and spacetime curvature.

Theorem 04 · Equilibrium Invariant
$$\nabla_\rho \Phi(\rho) = 1 \quad \text{and} \quad \Delta_x \Psi(x) = 0$$

Coherence equilibrium requires simultaneous unit-stable configuration and stationary representation. When both hold, geometry is not added as an external container — geometry is what coherence equilibrium looks like from within.

Theorem 05 · Resonant Interconnection
$$\Omega(\Delta\Psi_A + \Delta\Psi_B) \approx 1$$

Between two separate integrators with compatible phase structures, interference is constructive along specific relational channels. The target is 1, not 0: a living equilibrium recognized by the substrate as a single unified pattern, scaling with internal coherence rather than signal strength.

What would count against this architecture

A framework with no failure conditions is not strong — it is empty. These are the explicit conditions under which this architecture fails:

  • Drift: The internal chart requires continual revision to agree with itself; each cycle requires an ad-hoc accommodation.
  • Brittleness: Coherence that holds only in isolation and breaks upon contact with other integrators or physical measurements.
  • Narrowing: The space of admissible differences shrinks over iterations, indicating loss of degrees of freedom rather than stability.
  • Divergence of scales: The formal account holds at one scale but contradicts observations at another (e.g. chart reporting equilibrium while tissue decoheres).
