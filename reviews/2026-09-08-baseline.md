## Follow-up findings

### 1. Reconcile the calendar explanations — medium priority

The protocol overview currently describes a lengthened day, then says the day is an ordinary rotation with five days outside the count. The calendar article instead describes dates that can contain two sunrises. These are three different accounts of the remainder.

Reproduce: open `#/protocol`, read the calendar callout in Overview, then follow the 360° calendar link and compare the article's explanation.

Recommended next action: establish which description is authoritative, update the authored document at `documents/holographic-repolymerization.html`, rebuild documents.js, and check the linked article and search text for consistency. This review does not decide which scientific model is correct.

### 2. Correct the mobile navigation label — medium priority

At phone width, open the menu and choose Library. The menu closes and aria-expanded becomes false, but the button remains labelled Close navigation. This gives assistive technology an incorrect description of the next action.

The router in app.js hides the menu and resets its expanded state without resetting its label. Recommended correction: centralize menu close behavior or reset the label to Open navigation when routing closes it.

### 3. Match the homepage count to visible collections — low priority

The homepage says Six ways into the work, but its collection grid contains five cards: Research, Regenesis, The Ethos, The Lab, and Learning. The numbering jumps from 03 to 05 because Transmissions is hidden, and the Protocol is separately featured.

Recommended correction: derive the count and displayed numbering from the visible collection list, or use wording that does not assert a fixed count. Preserve the deliberate hidden state of Transmissions unless the user requests otherwise.

### 4. Align the calendar weekday example with its grid — low priority

The article's Structure paragraph says the fourteenth is Realis. The rendered calendar labels day 14 SEL (Selecta) and day 15 REA (Realis).

Recommended correction: align the prose example with the intended weekday sequence and update its stored search text at the same time.

## Suggested design refinement

The opening statement is distinctive, but abstract for a first-time reader. The page offers both Begin with the Ethos and A place to begin. A future editorial pass could make the recommended starting path more explicit and briefly describe what readers will find. This is a design recommendation, not a demonstrated functional defect.
